1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small data files that are placed on your computer or mobile device when you visit a website. Cookies are widely used by website owners to make their websites work, or to work more efficiently, as well as to provide reporting information.

Cookies set by the website owner (in this case, Raw Wealthy) are called "first-party cookies." Cookies set by parties other than the website owner are called "third-party cookies." Third-party cookies enable third-party features or functionality to be provided on or through the website (e.g., advertising, interactive content, and analytics).

2. Why We Use Cookies

We use cookies for several reasons:

2.1 Essential Cookies

These cookies are strictly necessary to provide you with services available through our Platform and to use some of its features, such as access to secure areas.

2.2 Performance and Analytics Cookies

These cookies are used to collect information about traffic to our Platform and how users use the Platform. The information gathered does not identify any individual visitor.

2.3 Functionality Cookies

These cookies allow our Platform to remember choices you make (such as your username, language, or the region you are in) and provide enhanced, more personal features.

2.4 Targeting and Advertising Cookies

These cookies track your browsing habits to enable us to show advertising which is more likely to be of interest to you.

5. How to Control Cookies

5.1 Browser Controls

You can set or amend your web browser controls to accept or refuse cookies. If you choose to reject cookies, you may still use our Platform though your access to some functionality and areas may be restricted.

5.2 How to Disable Cookies in Popular Browsers:

  • Google Chrome: Settings → Privacy and Security → Cookies and other site data
  • Mozilla Firefox: Options → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data
  • Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Cookies and website data
  • Microsoft Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions → Cookies and site data

5.3 Do Not Track Signals

Some browsers have a "Do Not Track" feature that lets you tell websites that you do not want to have your online activities tracked. Our Platform does not currently respond to "Do Not Track" signals.

6. Other Tracking Technologies

In addition to cookies, we may use other similar technologies:

6.1 Web Beacons

Small graphic images that may be included on our Platform and in our emails to understand user behavior.

6.2 Local Storage

HTML5 local storage is used to store larger amounts of data on your device, similar to cookies but with greater capacity.

6.3 Session Storage

Similar to local storage but limited to a single browsing session.
