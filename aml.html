Raw Wealthy

Anti-Money Laundering Policy

Last Updated: January 15, 2024

Compliance Statement: Raw Wealthy is committed to preventing money laundering and terrorist financing activities. We comply with all applicable anti-money laundering (AML) and counter-terrorist financing (CTF) regulations.

1. Policy Overview

This Anti-Money Laundering (AML) Policy outlines our commitment and procedures to prevent money laundering, terrorist financing, and other illegal activities through our Platform.

1.1 Objectives

Our AML program aims to:

  • Prevent the Platform from being used for money laundering or terrorist financing
  • Ensure compliance with all applicable AML/CTF laws and regulations
  • Protect our users and the integrity of our Platform
  • Implement robust customer due diligence procedures
  • Establish suspicious activity monitoring and reporting mechanisms

2. Customer Due Diligence (CDD)

We implement a risk-based approach to customer due diligence. All users must complete verification before conducting certain transactions.

2.1 Identification Requirements

We require the following from all users:

  • Full legal name and date of birth
  • Residential address
  • Government-issued photo identification
  • Proof of address (utility bill, bank statement)
  • Source of funds verification

2.2 Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD)

Enhanced due diligence is required for:

  • High-value transactions (above regulatory thresholds)
  • Politically Exposed Persons (PEPs)
  • Users from high-risk jurisdictions
  • Unusual transaction patterns

3. Ongoing Monitoring

We continuously monitor user activity for suspicious behavior. Our monitoring systems track:

Red Flag Indicators: Large, unusual, or suspicious transactions
Red Flag Indicators: Rapid movement of funds without clear business purpose
Red Flag Indicators: Use of multiple accounts or third parties
Red Flag Indicators: Attempts to avoid reporting thresholds

4. Transaction Monitoring

4.1 Transaction Limits

We implement the following transaction limits:

  • Daily deposit limit: ₦500,000
  • Daily withdrawal limit: ₦500,000
  • Monthly transaction limit: ₦5,000,000

Transaction Reporting: Transactions exceeding ₦5,000,000 or equivalent in foreign currency are reported to the appropriate regulatory authorities as required by law.

4.2 Source of Funds Verification

Users may be required to provide documentation verifying the source of their funds, including:

  • Bank statements
  • Pay slips or employment contracts
  • Business registration documents
  • Investment account statements
  • Gift or inheritance documentation

5. Record Keeping

We maintain comprehensive records as required by law:

5.1 Retention Period

All records are retained for a minimum of 5 years from the date of:

  • Account closure
  • Last transaction
  • Business relationship termination

5.2 Record Types

We maintain records of:

  • Customer identification documents
  • Account files and business correspondence
  • Transaction records and statements
  • Suspicious activity reports
  • Training records and compliance documentation

6. Suspicious Activity Reporting

We have established procedures for identifying and reporting suspicious activities:

6.1 Internal Reporting

All employees are trained to recognize and report suspicious activities to the Compliance Officer.

6.2 Regulatory Reporting

Suspicious Activity Reports (SARs) are filed with the appropriate regulatory authorities when required by law.

6.3 Confidentiality

All suspicious activity reports are treated with strict confidentiality. No disclosure is made to the subject of the report.

7. Sanctions Compliance

We screen all users against global sanctions lists, including:

  • United Nations Security Council sanctions lists
  • Office of Foreign Assets Control (OFAC) sanctions
  • European Union sanctions lists
  • Local regulatory sanctions lists

8. Employee Training

All employees receive regular AML/CTF training, including:

  • Recognition of suspicious activities
  • Customer due diligence procedures
  • Legal and regulatory requirements
  • Reporting obligations

9. Third-Party Relationships

We conduct due diligence on third-party service providers and ensure they have adequate AML controls in place.

10. User Responsibilities

As a user of Raw Wealthy, you agree to:

  • Provide accurate and complete information
  • Cooperate with verification requests
  • Report any errors or suspicious activities
  • Comply with all applicable laws and regulations
  • Not use the Platform for illegal purposes

11. Policy Review and Updates

This AML Policy is reviewed annually and updated as needed to comply with changing regulations and industry standards.
