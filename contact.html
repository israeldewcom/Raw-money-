Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to get a response?
We aim to respond to all inquiries within 24 hours during business days. Urgent matters are prioritized and typically receive a response within 4-6 hours.
What information should I provide when contacting support?
Please provide your account email, a clear description of the issue, and any relevant transaction IDs or screenshots. This helps us assist you more efficiently.
Do you have physical offices I can visit?
Our headquarters are located in Lagos, Nigeria. However, all support is handled online or via phone. Physical visits are by appointment only due to security protocols.
Can I speak to a specific department directly?
Yes, you can use the department-specific contact information above, or your inquiry will be routed to the appropriate team based on the subject you select.
