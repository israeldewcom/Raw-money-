Frequently Asked Questions

What is the hiring process like?
Our hiring process typically includes: 1) Initial resume screening, 2) Phone screening with HR, 3) Technical/role-specific assessment, 4) Interviews with team members, 5) Final interview with leadership, 6) Reference checks, and 7) Offer.
Do you offer remote work options?
Yes, we offer flexible work arrangements. Many roles are fully remote, while others are hybrid or office-based depending on the position requirements and team needs.
What benefits do you offer?
We offer comprehensive benefits including competitive salary, health insurance, retirement plans, annual bonuses, equity options, generous vacation time, professional development budget, and wellness programs.
Do you sponsor work visas?
We sponsor work visas for exceptional candidates in key roles. However, our primary focus is on candidates already authorized to work in our operating countries.
What is the typical response time after applying?
We aim to review applications within 1-2 weeks and will contact shortlisted candidates within that timeframe. Due to the volume of applications, we may not be able to respond to all applicants.
