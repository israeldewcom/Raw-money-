1. Understanding Investment Risks

All investments carry risk. The degree of risk varies based on the investment type, market conditions, and other factors. Below are key risks you should understand:

Capital Loss Risk

You could lose some or all of your invested capital. There is no guarantee of returns or principal protection.

Market Risk

Investment values fluctuate based on market conditions. Raw material prices are volatile and can change rapidly.

Liquidity Risk

Some investments may not be easily sold or converted to cash. Withdrawal restrictions may apply during certain periods.
